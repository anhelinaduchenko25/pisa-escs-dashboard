Does family background decide school results?

In every country, students from wealthier, better-educated families tend to score higher in PISA. But the strength of that link differs a lot. Where is school success most tied to the family you were born into, and where do school systems level the field?

5. What this dashboard does not show

  • A link is not a cause: ESCS and scores move together, but this does not prove how or why.
  • A weak link can also mean that everyone scores low (group D), not that the system is better.
  • ESCS is a composite index; countries differ in sampling and in how many students have ESCS data.
  • Some participants are regions or cities, not whole countries (marked on the map).
  • Group borders are fuzzy: countries marked "borderline" are within 2 standard errors of a threshold.
Data: OECD PISA 2025 Database, student questionnaire file; calculations by our team (weighted, 10 plausible values, BRR-Fay replicate weights). Verification against OECD, PISA 2025 Results (Volume I).
